# Nekuna

Small games, made to be replayed.

Nekuna is an independent game studio. We make free-to-play games
that are easy to pick up, fair by design, and playable anywhere —
on web, iOS, Android and TikTok.

## What we've made

**Jelly Drop** — a relaxed physics merge game. Drop and slingshot
planets around a gravity well, merge two of a kind, and climb the
chain from Moon to Pulsar to black hole. Play it at nekuna.com.
